- Sessions are grouped by project, under labels that stay in view as you scroll, and every card shows its agent's mark.
- A session's running subagents are rows on its card: what each was asked to do, what it is doing and its state.
- While a session is blocked, a bar along the bottom names it and its project. Press it to bring that card into view, and again for the next. TalkBack says when a session becomes blocked.
- Each usage block shows how many sessions its agent has.
